Mark Hand — Reply (B Corp question) Sent 2026-08-04

mark.c.hand@gmail.com | The Stakehold newsletter, UT Arlington

No rush at all on the timing. I don't have a specific launch date planned but I'm steadily adding stores and products. I can let you know when something comes up that would make for a good moment. Maybe if I unearth a rich vein of co-ops in the next month or so that would be a good occasion.

I looked into the B Corp marketplace idea. It would actually be easier to build in some ways. B Lab maintains a public directory of certified B Corps, so identifying companies is straightforward compared to "worker-owned." Filter to the ones with consumer-facing online stores and you'd probably have 500-1,000 shops. The scraping tech is the same. It would be a bigger site.

My hesitation is that it dilutes the mission I have in mind. I want workerowned.info to show that worker ownership specifically is a viable strategy. There are real obstacles that worker-owned companies face from shareholder-driven competition, and I think organization and good tools can help beat that. Expanding to B Corps or benefit corporations might dilute that. I know I know we can't let perfect be the enemy of good as they say, but I'd like to give workerowned.info a little more time and see how far I can take it before branching out.

That said, keep pestering me about it and I'll fold eventually. I can see a scenario where workerowned hits a ceiling because we just can't fill in some gaps (e.g. electronics, services), and including B-corp is too attractive to keep out.
